Who We Are

CRAFT. STYLE. COMMUNITY.

7 Star Barber Shop is Waltham's go-to spot for precision cuts, clean fades, expert braids, and top-tier beard care. We pride ourselves on quality work and a welcoming environment for everyone — from kids getting their first cut to regulars who've trusted us for years.

Our stylists — including Evelyn, a beloved long-time favorite — bring skill, care, and personality to every chair. Come in, relax, and leave looking your best.

Walk-ins are always welcome, but appointments are recommended so we can give you the attention you deserve.
